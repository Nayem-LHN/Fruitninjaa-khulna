About the Juice Corner

A tiny shop, a huge crush on fresh fruit

Fruitninjaa (ফ্রুট নিনজা) sits right across Khulna City Medical College Hospital on Sher-E-Bangla Road — a small, hard-working juice bar where every glass is prepared in front of you from fruit that is ripe, real and never pre-mixed.

Regulars rave about the authentic taste of Mint Lemon — a zesty, cooling blast for hot afternoons — and Zeera Pani, the tangy, spiced drink that aids digestion. Day and night the corner stays lively, serving everything from creamy Banana Smoothies and Nut Shakes to an indulgent Special Faluda.

Fresh, affordable and quick — ৳1–200 per person — Fruitninjaa delivers a guilt-free burst of natural energy to Khulna’s food scene.
